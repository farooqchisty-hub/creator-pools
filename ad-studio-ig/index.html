How the score works (out of 100). AI share 35: share of stored and fresh posts about AI video, ads or tools. Competitor proof 20: paid partnership, collab post or brand tag 20, affiliate link or code 15, partnership the creator told us about but no post found 14, demo or tutorial 10, plus 3 for ad and video tools like Higgsfield, HeyGen, Arcads, InVideo. Reel performance 20: median plays of the last 24 reels compared with followers, full marks at 40%. Format 15: on-camera presenter who demos tools scores highest, faceless scores 0. Audience 10: marketers, ecom owners and founders score highest. Minus 10 if the last post is more than 45 days old, minus 25 past 90 days.

Sources. Stored posts and transcripts from the creator engine, an LLM read of each creator's posts, and a fresh ScrapeCreators pull of the last 24 reels plus posts that tag them. Fee bands are the engine's own suggested fee to authorised max for their size. Top 30 are numbered in green; the rest are backups.

Ecom lean (0 to 10). A second LLM read of each creator's posts and last 16 reels, judging whether their audience is store owners, DTC founders or performance marketers. 9 to 10 means content made explicitly for them (Shopify, Meta ads, ROAS, product and UGC ads). The Ecom / DTC shortlist is everyone at 8 or above, plus anyone at 7 who also scores 67 or more overall. With that filter on, rows sort by ecom fit: half the overall score plus five times the ecom lean.
