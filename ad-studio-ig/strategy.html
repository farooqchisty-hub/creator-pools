2. Hard gates (fail any one and the creator is out)

  1. English on camera. The creator speaks English in their reels. We check transcripts, not bios.
  2. Audience in Ad Studio's markets. Ad Studio runs in US, CA, GB, DE, FR, IT and ES. Default: the creator's country and audience have to lean to those markets, using your tier-1 rule from the re-offer program (US, CA, GB, IE, AU, DE, FR, IT). Unknown country holds the creator for review. This is the one open decision, see section 8.
  3. Proven work for a top-10 competitor. At least one post that walks through one of these tools: Higgsfield, Runway, InVideo, HeyGen, Arcads, Creatify, Captions, Topview, AdCreative.ai, Synthesia. Model names on their own (Kling, Veo, Seedance) count only when used inside one of these tools.
  4. A real person on camera. Of their last 12 reels, at least half show the creator's face or voice presenting. The competitor post itself has to be the creator walking through the product and giving their take. Feeds that are mostly AI-generated clips with music are out.
  5. Active. Posted in the last 30 days, and at least 5K followers.

6. What we pay: $300 to $1,200

The package for every creator: one Reel of 45 to 90 seconds, on camera, in English. They walk through Ad Studio live (paste a store URL, get ready-to-run ads) and give their honest take. It goes out as a collab post with the Brevo account, with a comment-keyword call to action ("Comment ADS") that sends a tracked link. We also get 60 days of paid usage to run it as a partnership ad in the Ad Studio Meta campaign, plus the raw footage.

If they ask more for usage rights, we can move up to the walk-away. Above it, we hold and ask for recent reach numbers, and we never threaten to walk.

Budget if all 50 close: roughly $20K at opening offers to $33K at walk-away, assuming most land in the $400 to $900 bands. I'll give the exact total once the 50 are scored.

Payment terms (your standing rule): one payment, no advance and no split. It's released through Tolt after we approve the final video, and lands in 3 to 5 business days. They don't publish until we send the go-ahead with the tracked link.

7. How we close them

  1. New engine campaign: "Ad Studio IG". It gets its own brief, offer ladder and sequence, kept separate from the Brevo ecom campaign. The 64 creators who ignored the ecom pitch get a fresh angle: a product that matches what they already make.
  2. Wave 1: your 4 favorites, the warm creators (6 accepted and 2 interested or negotiating on Brevo ecom deals) and the top 10. I write every opener. You approve them before anything goes out.
  3. The opener (90 words max, your outbound voice): name their actual competitor reel ("your Higgsfield walkthrough"), one line on Ad Studio, the number and the package up front, and the Tolt terms. Email first (81% have one), Instagram DM 48 hours later if there's no reply.
  4. Follow-ups on your pursuit cadence: every 2 days, 3 times, then the creator goes to unresponsive. The engine handles negotiation within the ladder.
  5. Waves 2 and 3: the rest of the 50 in batches of about 15, after we see wave 1's reply rate and the counters that come back.
  6. Delivery: script outline, then the final video, then our approval, then the go-ahead with the tracked link, then publish, then payout. We track sign-ups per creator on the custom pixel event.

8. Open decision

The market gate. Many of the strongest AI-tool creators in the database speak English on camera but are based in India, and so is their audience. Ad Studio doesn't run there. The default is a hard tier-1 gate, matching your re-offer rule. The alternative is English on camera from any country, which gives a bigger pool but less of the audience can buy Ad Studio.
