Why the same list costs 5x more on one platform

Two pricing models, very different bills. Most platforms price by contacts stored: every profile on your list costs money every month whether you email them or not. Brevo prices by emails sent with unlimited contacts, which is why the gap widens as lists grow faster than sending does.

The hidden line items live off the pricing page. Unsubscribed contacts that still count toward your tier, suppressed profiles billed until manually removed, overage fees, per-1,000-contact add-ons, transactional email sold as a separate product. The notes column flags the ones that bite most often.

Model 36 months, not month one. A growing store's list compounds. A platform that is $40 cheaper today but prices per contact can be thousands more expensive over the horizon you will actually stay on it. That is what the 36-month column is for.

Agencies: this is the client-facing artifact. Platform recommendations land better with a cost table at the client's own scale than with a feature matrix. Run it at current size and at the 12-month growth target.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Klaviyo or Mailchimp get expensive as my list grows?
They price by contacts stored, so every subscriber costs money monthly whether you email them or not, and unsubscribed or inactive profiles can still count toward your tier. Volume-priced platforms only charge for what you send.
Are these the exact prices I will pay?
No. They are estimates built from each vendor's published list prices for their mainstream marketing tier as of August 2026, interpolated between public pricing points. Promotions, annual discounts and enterprise deals change the numbers; treat this as a like-for-like comparison, then confirm on vendor pricing pages.
What add-on costs do pricing pages hide?
The common ones: transactional email sold as a separate product, SMS credits billed on top, charges for unsubscribed or suppressed contacts, per-1,000-contact fees above your tier, and overage rates when you exceed a send cap.
What does switching platforms actually involve?
Exporting contacts and suppression lists, rebuilding automations, re-authenticating your domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and warming up sending. Most stores complete it in one to two weeks. The gated migration plan above is a step-by-step checklist.
