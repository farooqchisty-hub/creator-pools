The finding

Our entries are generic. The questions are not.

On 15 of 26 pages where Brevo appears but the engine ignored us, our placement is already good, sometimes rank #1, in the intro, in a heading. Position is not the problem.

What differs is content. The prompts name a platform: BigCommerce, Magento, Squarespace. On emailtooltester.com we are the number one listed tool and our entry names no ecommerce platform at all. On insiderone.com, a Magento query, our entry names none while Klaviyo's names Shopify. Three of five pages tested show the same shape.

So when someone asks an engine for the best email tool for a BigCommerce store, nothing in our entry connects us to the question, however high up the page we sit. That is a content fix on pages we already own a position on, not a link acquisition problem.

Caveat: n=5, pilot sweep. The 346-prompt run is what confirms it.
