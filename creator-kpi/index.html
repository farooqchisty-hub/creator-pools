Creator & Influencer KPIs

Campaign
Platform

Funnel: from views to installs

Reach is video views (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) plus LinkedIn impressions where the creator reported them. Site visits are pushowl.com sessions plus brevo.com visitors from tracked creator links.

Paid out by creator

Amount paid out in USD, one bar per creator per campaign wave. Colour is the platform of the paid placement.

CPM by creator

Paid out per 1,000 views or impressions. Log scale: values run from under $2 to over $10,000. Creators with no reported reach are left out.

Site visits by creator

pushowl.com sessions (Omni, PushOwl GA4) and brevo.com visitors (Omni, Brevo web tracker).

Cost per site visit

Paid out divided by pushowl.com sessions plus brevo.com visitors. Creators with zero visits are listed in the table as no visits.

pushowl.com sessions per day

Sessions from creator UTMs, stacked by platform.

brevo.com visitors per day

LinkedIn seeding links land on brevo.com. Klaviyo list tax page CTAs forward their UTMs to Brevo signup.

Engagement rate by creator

Likes plus comments divided by views or impressions. Shares and saves are not public, so they are not counted.

Paid out by payment status

From the tracker's payment column. "Blocked" means the creator has not added a payout method in Tolt.

Platform scorecard

CPM and cost per visit are blended: total paid out divided by total reach or visits, counting only creators who have that metric.
