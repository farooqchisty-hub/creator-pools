Where the deals leaked

  1. We went quiet after they said yes. 40 threads. The engine promises "final approval, campaign brief, angle and timeline" after agreement, but none of these exists as something it can send, so agreed creators wait and the engine keeps apologising. @foundedceo agreed $500 on 3 Oct and has been holding production for 10 days.
  2. We were slow on warm threads. 65 threads. Replies and counters waited days; 26 drafts are still held for approval past 48 hours.
  3. We anchored far below a fair price. 44 threads. Example: @apcorn (9,079 median views) said $500; we opened at $100 and lost him. $500 is $55 per 1,000 views, inside what we already pay.
  4. Process friction before value. 16 threads: asking for an email, screenshots or Tolt sign-up before sending the brief or a number.

Benchmark used below: across our 54 closed deals we paid a median of $21 per 1,000 median views, $47 at the 75th percentile and $65 at the 90th.

Engine fixes so this stops leaking

  1. Delivery kit on agreement. The moment a fee is agreed, the engine sends the brief, angle, timeline and approval steps automatically, with Tolt in the same message. Needs the kit content once.
  2. Meet-the-ask rule in code. If their stated price is at or under $65 per 1,000 median views and within $1,500, accept it instead of opening low.
  3. 24-hour clock on warm threads. Anyone who replied gets an answer within 24 hours; held drafts older than 24 hours go to you in a daily digest instead of sitting.
  4. Value before admin. Never ask for an email, screenshots or Tolt before the creator has the brief and a number.
