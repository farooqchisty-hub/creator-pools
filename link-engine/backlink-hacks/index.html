3. What round 1 taught us

  1. Most creator hacks are stale. Of 75 mechanisms whose links could be read, about 42 (56%) are nofollow, ugc or redirect-wrapped. Read rel in the raw HTML before doing anything.
  2. True hacks on generic platforms are filler for a DR 90 brand. One new low-relevance domain does not move 91k. Keep them for new sites, or do them once.
  3. The biggest wins are Brevo's own leaks. Dead URLs, the apex 404, legacy domains and a noindexed listing strand about 9k referring domains (the sum across URLs). Fixing them needs redirects and settings, no outreach.
  4. Jackpots scale with the customer base. Things customers paste onto their own sites earn thousands of domains: legal clauses, sub-processor lists, form badges. Brevo's German privacy policy already has 16,718 dofollow referring domains from exactly this.
  5. The best hit rate comes from one signal: a rival is linked on a host and Brevo is not. Developer docs, marketplaces and partner pages show it most. Brevo lags Resend and SendGrid most in the developer ecosystem.
  6. Look for multipliers. One action can repeat across many pages: docs versions, awesome-list mirrors, auto-generated "alternatives" pages, one page per action. Score a hack by links per action, not per platform.

8. Method, files, cost

  • Research: three parallel passes collected 98 mechanisms (40 social, 29 forums, 29 GitHub and web). Each pass read the live rel on its examples.
  • Re-check: every round-1 verdict was re-checked here. Rendered pages (Stripe Apps) went through a headless scrape.
  • Backlink data: DataForSEO via treg. Its domain rank is on a 0 to 1000 scale. "RD-sum" adds up referring domains across URLs, so domains linking to several dead URLs count more than once.
  • Files: brevo_redirect_map.csv (300 rows: linked URL, where it lands now, referring domains, suggested 301 target, confidence). Help-center rows answered 403 to our checker, so confirm them before redirecting.
  • Reusable validator: ~/claude fc/backlink-hacks/validator/ (gates T1 to T5, batch mode).
  • Data cost: under $12 on treg (the team's total for the day).
