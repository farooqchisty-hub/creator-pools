02Is this a retargeting account?

The Transparency Center never discloses targeting or audience lists, so no public source can prove this either way. The clearest test case in the set is creative CR02009282397962502145, highlighted in the table above: an 8:49 changelog walkthrough landing on a help-center article, served in India only, under 1,000 impressions. Its shape says a great deal about how this account buys.

Most likely site-visitor remarketing, on a very small budget

Inference, not disclosure

Points to retargeting

  • No signup is required to be on the list. Google remarketing fires on a page view. Omnisend's container runs nine live Google Ads conversion tags on conversion ID 823857736 plus a conversion linker, so a single visit to omnisend.com with ad-personalization consent is enough to enter their visitor audience. Never having created an account rules out Customer Match, which needs an email address. It does not rule out remarketing.
  • The creative is a retention asset. An 8:49 changelog walkthrough landing on a help-center article is made for people who already know the product. Their actual prospecting videos run 0:22 to 0:35 and land on /migrate-from-klaviyo/, /demo/ or the homepage.
  • They do not prospect in India. Zero of 42 live search ads serve India. A brand that excludes a market from search would not open a broad YouTube prospecting campaign there, but an audience-based campaign follows the person wherever they happen to be.
  • The reach shrank as the audience narrowed. The same monthly series ran in 38 markets at 3,000 to 4,000 impressions a year ago and is now one market under 1,000. That is the shape of a list, not of a geo buy.

The competing explanation

  • A cheap Demand Gen buy looks identical from outside. A low-budget YouTube in-feed campaign with broad or interest targeting produces the same signature: serving concentrated in one low-CPM market, impressions under the reporting threshold, no other markets recorded.
  • India-only may be an artifact of low volume. The Transparency Center lists markets where impressions were actually recorded. A campaign too small to register elsewhere shows up as one country even when the targeting is global.
  • Interest targeting would catch the same people. The video is keyworded for klaviyo alternative, Claude MCP integration and Model Context Protocol. Anyone with that watch history is in-market for it without ever having touched omnisend.com.

How to settle it in under a minute

  1. On the ad itself: the ⓘ or three-dot control on a YouTube ad opens “Why this ad?”. Google states the reason there, and “you visited the advertiser's website or app” is the disclosure that confirms remarketing outright.
  2. In My Ad Center: open myadcenter.google.com and check the advertiser list. Omnisend appearing there means they are reaching you through an audience, not a keyword.
  3. In the cookie jar: a _gcl_au cookie set on omnisend.com is the Google Ads linker, and it is the mechanism that carries a visitor into the list.
