The model

Your correction was the load-bearing one: agency owners have relevant audiences that do not press like, because their followers are store owners rather than marketers. So engagement is now a liveness check in the gate and nothing else. It sets no price.

score   = sqrt( min(followers, 50,000) ) x (on_topic / 10)
ceiling = $350 x score / score_orsi        rounded to $25, capped at $1,000, floored at $100

Three things to notice:

  • The square root is the cooling, and it is literally "at least half": it halves the exponent on audience size, so ten times the following is worth about three times the money rather than ten.
  • Relevance is the multiplier that moves the number. A 20k feed that is 9 of 10 on topic now outranks a 135k feed that is 4 of 10.
  • The cap at 50,000 exists because past that a LinkedIn following stops being ecommerce operators and starts being LinkedIn.

The model landed on your number without being told it. You priced exactly one person by hand: Orsi at $350. Anchoring the middle there puts the strongest profile in the pool at $1,003, which rounds to the $1,000 ceiling you had asked for separately. Nothing was tuned to make that happen.

The identity problem

Two of eleven repliers stopped over who we appear to be, and the domain is only half of it. Instantly was sending under its own per-inbox display names, so recipients saw From: "Michael King" <michael@pioneservice.org> over a signature reading "Farooq Chisty, Ecom Marketing, Brevo". Three identities in one email is the shape of a phishing attempt. One thread had a fourth: a stale config sent Ben Zettler a message from creators@magicfitapp.com, which belongs to the creator engine.

Cold sending is paused as of 17 Sep. Replies are unaffected, they use a separate ceiling. The stale config is fixed. The route to your own mailbox is documented in tools/brevo-mailbox/README.md: brevo.com runs on Google Workspace, so the Gmail API is the only path that passes SPF, and the OAuth client already exists from the creator engine. It is one command for you.
