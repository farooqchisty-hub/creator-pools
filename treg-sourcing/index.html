The short version. treg is 4.5x more expensive per emailable head than Apify and it is still the right move, because the engine's problem has never been volume. It has 12,380 creators and 670 qualified. On the 8 September calendar, ranks 11 to 40 of the bulk cohort returned 0 of 30 in ICP. treg's rows arrive with the quantitative half of the ICP already true, which is the half the engine currently pays ScrapeCreators to discover after the fact.

3. Discovery, measured

All three routes were run against the engine's live ICP and deduped against the 4,018 handles already in the database.

3a. exa, semantic

Five queries, 25 results each, $0.110 total. 125 results collapsed to 65 unique handles, of which 8 were already in the engine and 57 were new. That is $0.0019 per new handle, the cheapest discovery of anything tested.

The 8 duplicates are the signal worth reading: itskatiefarrell, theecommerceassistant, growthbysabir, allaboutemailmarketing_ and four others were all creators the engine had independently found and validated. exa re-derived them from a sentence.

Query shape decides everything. Queries written as "creators who teach Shopify store owners about X" returned educators. The one query written as "creators who run a small online store and post behind the scenes" returned 21 straight brand and boutique accounts (pangooapparel, shop.olivelynn, atianasboutique), which the qualifier rejects as company voice. Two of the 57 were URL parsing artifacts, success and inspiration.

3b. influencers.club, structured

Six keyword sets, each with number_of_followers {800, 20000}, engagement_percent {min 1.5}, profile_language ["en"]. 113 results for 1.13 credits, which is $0.676, yielding 107 new handles. That is $0.0063 per new handle, and unlike exa every one of them is already inside the follower band, above the engagement floor and in an in-scope language.

Median of the 107: 9,101 followers, 2.16% engagement. The engine's own measured ICP pattern is a median of 2,336 followers at 2.6% engagement, so the band should be tightened to roughly 800 to 8,000 on the next run.

The tight keywords return small pools. 13 creators for klaviyo means structured search alone cannot feed a 40 a day cold lane. It has to be paired with exa for recall.

3c. Lookalike, do not buy it

This reproduces the exact failure the Apify follow-graph already has. Five seeds drawn from creators the qualifier passed, 20 lookalikes each, $0.598.

Without an engagement filter the 54 new rows ran 0.00% to 1.38% engagement and were dominated by agencies and app companies: Debutify, Kangaroo Rewards, Shopanova, Xuperb Digital Marketing, AppLabx, Developerz Hub, Prime Ecommerce Solutions. The engine's own sourcing comments already record that seeding by agency fills the graph with more agencies.

Adding engagement_percent {min 2} to fix that collapsed the pool from 50,360 to 3,045 and destroyed topical relevance entirely. The 20 rows returned for the theecommerceassistant seed were a DJ, a graphic designer, an artist and assorted personal accounts with no ecommerce connection. Similarity and engagement cannot both be satisfied on this route.

The external_url gap is load bearing. The engine reads external_url and bio_links to crawl the link in bio, which is where a large share of creator emails actually live. Two ways round it: send X-Treg-Route-Prefer: scrapecreators on the profile call and pay $0.00188 instead of $0.001, or use influencersclub.creators.enrich.raw at $0.0179, which returns links_in_bio plus 12 captioned posts in a single call and replaces both the profile and the posts fetch.

6. The five open engine wounds this closes

  1. The role mailbox jam. Incidents 1082 and 1146. Eight approved Gmail drafts, every one a support@, press@, media@ or clientsupport@ address, with no reject route to retire them, so the Gmail lane reports work it must not do. email_type classifies these at source. Four of my seventeen emails were role_based_email and would never have entered the queue. src/emailquality.js SYSTEM_ROLE currently matches none of those prefixes, and extending that regex is free whether or not treg ships.
  2. The greeting gate. Most Gmail recontact leads carry name: null, so the standing rule is to default to "Hello". enrich.profile returns first_name as a separate field.
  3. The proof of work gate blocking follow-ups. Incidents 1083 and 1308, eighteen due follow-ups refused because the lead holds no real post. The documented fix is POST /api/discover/enrich at seven ScrapeCreators credits per creator. enrich.raw puts twelve captioned posts on file for $0.0179 in one call.
  4. Single provider dependency. The key configured in this machine's scrape-creators MCP server is at zero credits right now. The engine's own key at ~/.scrapecreators_key has 11,780 left, so nothing is broken, but the engine has one key and no failover. treg.instagram.user.profile waterfalls eleven providers.
  5. Both cold channels dry. Since 8 September the Instagram calendar crosses out of the good pool at rank 11 and Gmail has the same eight role mailboxes. Section 7 refills the pool with rows that are in band before they are ever scored.

A runner/treg_source.py beside the existing apify_source.py, four stages, cheapest gate first.

8. What treg does not fix

Bio keyword matching still admits agencies. Reading all twenty enriched rows by hand, roughly six to eight are genuine creator educators. The rest are build agencies (FDRY, Numbered, Robusht), service sellers, a Shopify employee, an artist and a Waterloo engineering student whose bio happens to tag @shopify. treg lifts ICP precision from near zero on the bulk cohort to roughly a third. It does not make it clean. Stage 3 is not optional.

  • Klaviyo's own brand account came back as a result from an exa query. Vendor and competitor exclusion lists still need a human pass every sweep, the same rule the citation engine runs under.
  • Discovery pools are small at high precision. Thirteen creators matched klaviyo in the band. Precision and volume trade off directly here.
  • The MCP call tool drops the body for exa.creators.search and returns a 400 saying query is undefined. The treg CLI with the identical payload works. Use the CLI for exa until that is fixed.
  • The discovery route returned a transient 429 on the first attempt, with the text "Discovery API credit limit reached". It served normally minutes later with 8,781 credits showing. Any production runner needs a retry on that specific message rather than treating it as exhaustion.
  • Engagement percent is computed by influencers.club, not measured by the engine. The engine prices on median views and its own measured reach. Their number is a pre-filter, not a substitute for the evidence pass.

Option 2 drops Apify entirely and puts every email through enrich.profile, so every row arrives with email_type, first_name, engagement, language and last post date. The email step becomes 400 calls at $0.1196, which is $47.84 instead of $20.54, and the total lands near $65. That is above the $62.11 currently on the treg balance, so it needs a top up.

Option 3 hits exactly 60% and no more by skipping the email step on the last 190 accounts, which takes Option 1 down to roughly $29.

Proportion. The signups ladder opens at $500 and targets $600 to $1,000 with a $1,300 ceiling. The entire sourcing bill for 500 accounts is less than one closed signups deal. The binding constraint is not money, it is that the $5.00 daily Apify cap set on 28 August does not govern the treg balance, so somebody has to decide whether this is a single authorised spend or spread across a fortnight.

9e. What will go wrong

  • ICP survival is about 40% on the signups bands, better than the third measured on the micro band but still not clean. Every cost line above already carries that discount; removing the captions and scoring stage to save $12 would put 300 off-ICP accounts into the pipeline.
  • Shopify staff read as perfect ICP to every filter tested. Four came back in two small samples. The vendor and own-domain exclusion list needs a human pass, same rule the citation engine runs under.
  • The 1,290 unmeasured rows are mostly the sheet-import cohort and will not salvage at the rate assumed if the sample was unlucky. If they salvage at zero, the gap grows by about 50 accounts and the bill by about $4.
  • LinkedIn contributes no emails at all right now. Any LinkedIn share of the 500 counts against the 40% that may be email-free, not toward the 60%.

Appendix: how the bake-off was paid for

treg balance moved from $66.483 to $62.114, so $4.369 total: $0.110 exa, $0.676 structured search, $0.598 lookalike, $2.033 email yield test, $0.233 raw enrich, $0.719 head to head, reverse lookups and probes. Every endpoint was called with treg's injected keys, no key was held locally.
